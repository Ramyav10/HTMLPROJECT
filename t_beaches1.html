1. Chennai - Marina Beach

Marina Beach is the most iconic landmark of Chennai and the longest natural urban beach in India, stretching nearly 13 kilometers along the Bay of Bengal. Its wide sandy shores, lively promenade, and cultural significance make it one of the most visited beaches in the country. Every day, thousands of people gather here to enjoy the cool sea breeze, watch the waves, and soak in the vibrant atmosphere.

2. Rameswaram - Dhanushkodi Beach

Dhanushkodi Beach, located at the tip of Rameswaram in Tamil Nadu, is one of the most unique and mysterious coastal destinations in India. Often called the “Ghost Town”, it lies at the confluence of the Bay of Bengal and the Indian Ocean, offering breathtaking views and a hauntingly beautiful atmosphere.

3. Kanyakumari - Thiruvalluvar Statue

The Thiruvalluvar Statue rises majestically to a height of 133 feet, representing the 133 chapters of the Thirukkural, one of the greatest works of Tamil literature. Built on a rocky island next to the Vivekananda Rock Memorial, the statue is a tribute to the legendary Tamil poet and philosopher Thiruvalluvar, who preached values of virtue, wealth, and love. Visitors can reach the statue by ferry, and from its base, enjoy panoramic views of the endless ocean. The sheer scale of the monument, combined with the roar of the waves, makes it a powerful symbol of Tamil Nadu’s cultural pride.
