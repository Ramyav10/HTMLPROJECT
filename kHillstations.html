1.Munnar - The Jewel of the Western Ghats

Munnar, located in Kerala’s Idukki district, is one of India’s most famous hill stations, often called the "Kashmir of South India". Nestled at an altitude of around 1,600 meters, it is renowned for its rolling tea plantations, mist-covered valleys, and cool climate. The town was once a summer retreat for British officials, and today it attracts travelers seeking both relaxation and adventure.

2. Wayanad - Banasura Hill

Banasura Hill, located in the Wayanad district of Kerala, is one of the tallest peaks in the Western Ghats and a paradise for trekkers and nature lovers. Rising to about 6,800 feet, it offers breathtaking views of the surrounding valleys, forests, and the famous Banasura Sagar Dam, which is the largest earthen dam in India. The hill is named after the mythological demon king Banasura, adding a touch of legend to its natural grandeur.
