1. Ooty

Ooty, also known as Udhagamandalam, is the “Queen of Hill Stations” in Tamil Nadu, famous for its cool climate, tea plantations, lakes, gardens, and the UNESCO-listed Nilgiri Mountain Railway. It’s one of South India’s most popular tourist destinations, offering scenic beauty and cultural charm.

2. Kodaikanal

Kodaikanal, nestled in the Palani Hills of Tamil Nadu, is one of India’s most beloved hill stations. Known for its misty mountains, serene lakes, waterfalls, and lush forests, it offers a refreshing escape from the heat of the plains. Often called the “Princess of Hill Stations”, Kodaikanal is a perfect blend of natural beauty and tranquility.
