1. Nallamala Forests

The Nallamala Forests, stretching across the Eastern Ghats in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, are among the largest and most picturesque forest ranges in southern India. Known for their dense greenery, rolling hills, and rich biodiversity, these forests are home to the famous Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve, one of the largest tiger reserves in the country. The region shelters a wide variety of wildlife including tigers, leopards, sloth bears, wild dogs, and numerous bird species, making it a paradise for nature lovers and wildlife enthusiasts. Apart from its ecological importance, the Nallamala Forests hold deep spiritual significance, as the revered V, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, is nestled within this landscape. Trekking trails, scenic viewpoints, and the tranquil Krishna River flowing through the valleys add to the charm of this natural wonder. For travelers, the Nallamala Forests offer a rare blend of adventure, spirituality, and untouched wilderness, making them a must-visit destination in Andhra Pradesh.

2. Araku Valley

Araku Valley, nestled in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, is a breathtaking hill station known for its lush greenery, coffee plantations, and serene landscapes. Located about 120 km from Visakhapatnam, the valley is surrounded by thick forests, waterfalls, and tribal villages that showcase the region’s rich culture and traditions. Popular attractions include the Borra Caves, famous for their stunning stalactite and stalagmite formations, the Tribal Museum, which highlights the lifestyle and artistry of local tribes, and the scenic Padmapuram Gardens. The valley’s cool climate, winding ghat roads, and panoramic viewpoints make it a perfect getaway for nature lovers and adventure seekers. With its blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and aromatic coffee, Araku Valley offers visitors a refreshing escape into the heart of Andhra Pradesh’s highlands.
