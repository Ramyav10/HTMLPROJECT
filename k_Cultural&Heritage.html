1. Fort Kochi

Fort Kochi, located in the city of Kochi (Cochin), is one of Kerala’s most charming destinations, blending colonial history, coastal beauty, and vibrant culture. Once a hub for Portuguese, Dutch, and British traders, it still carries the legacy of centuries of maritime trade. Today, Fort Kochi is famous for its heritage streets, art festivals, and seaside atmosphere.

2. Backwaters of Alappuzha

Alappuzha (also called Alleppey) in Kerala is world-famous for its enchanting backwaters, a network of canals, lagoons, and lakes fringed with coconut palms. Often referred to as the “Venice of the East”, it is one of the most sought-after destinations in South India. The backwaters here offer a unique blend of natural beauty, traditional village life, and serene experiences that cannot be found anywhere else.
